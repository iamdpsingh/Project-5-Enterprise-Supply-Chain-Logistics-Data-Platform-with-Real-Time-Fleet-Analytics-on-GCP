# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

To measure the success of the platform and the supply chain, the following KPIs will be tracked via the Supply Chain Control Tower dashboards.

## 1. Executive KPIs
- **Total Orders & Shipments**: Volume processed over time.
- **On-Time Delivery (OTD) Rate**: Percentage of shipments delivered on or before the ETA.
- **Total Inventory Value**: Capital tied up in current inventory.
- **Overall Fleet Utilization**: Percentage of vehicles actively routing vs. idle.
- **Delayed Shipments Count**: Real-time count of shipments at risk of or currently experiencing delays.

## 2. Logistics KPIs
- **Delivery Performance**: Average delivery time per route/region.
- **Route Efficiency**: Planned vs. actual distance and time.
- **Warehouse Processing Time**: Average time from order receipt to dispatch.
- **Cost per Delivery**: Calculated fuel and operational cost per shipment.

## 3. Fleet & IoT KPIs
- **Active vs. Idle Vehicles**: Real-time operational state.
- **Average Fleet Speed & Violations**: Tracking driver safety and compliance.
- **Fuel Efficiency (MPG/KMPL)**: Fuel consumed relative to distance traveled.
- **Maintenance Alerts**: Count of critical engine temperature or diagnostic anomalies.

## 4. Inventory KPIs
- **Current Stock Levels**: Real-time view of inventory across warehouses.
- **Stockout Rate**: Frequency of products running out of stock.
- **Inventory Turnover Ratio**: How often inventory is sold and replaced.
- **Warehouse Capacity Utilization**: Percentage of physical space used.

## 5. Supplier KPIs
- **Supplier On-Time Percentage**: Reliability of suppliers meeting delivery windows.
- **Average Lead Time**: Time from purchase order to receipt.
- **Supplier Rating**: Aggregated score based on quality and delivery performance.
